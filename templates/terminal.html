EXPLORE
Drag the field or use arrow keys / WASD. Scroll or pinch to zoom into a particular region. + and − zoom at the center. The position map shows your viewport, never hidden groups.
GATHER
Hover over an unsettling number. Its entire irregular group grows and draws toward your pointer over 1.5 seconds. Stay nearby until the ring closes.
REFINE
Click the gathered group. Its numbers fly to the correct bin automatically. Fresh digits immediately fill their places, and a new group appears elsewhere in the region.
TOUCH / KEYBOARD
Hold a number to gather, release once ready to refine. Two fingers pan and pinch. F cycles signals already visible in the field; Enter or Space refines the focused group after gathering.
DIFFICULTY
Ordinary-number clicks are harmless on Normal, cost up to 50 points on Quota achiever, and give a strike on Refiner of the quarter. Three strikes end that file. Early gathering clicks never count as mistakes.
SAVE
Your signed file, camera, and replacement digits save locally. A timed shift keeps running while you are away. Reconnect within 24 hours to renew your save.
